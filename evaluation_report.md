**2.3 Rejection of Hidden Variables**
- **Document:** "Bell's theorem rules out theories where each particle carries independent information."
- **Assessment:** ✅ **Correct.** Bell's theorem specifically rules out local hidden variable theories that assume particles have independent properties determined at creation.
